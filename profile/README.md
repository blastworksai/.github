## What Blastworks is

Blastworks is one person, fifteen years in, with no agency layer.
I train teams to work with AI, and I build the tools that give people their time back.
Everything runs on tuned open source, at prices you can check.

My strength is translating: I speak to humans and to AI agents, and each has its own language.

## The mission

Anyone can now build what they can put into words.
What it really costs is time: learning, trying, failing.
Blastworks exists to buy that time back for people, so they can do the work only they can do.

Four things I don't compromise on:

1. **AI frees people up.** It takes the repetitive work off your desk; it doesn't replace your people.
2. **Open source is the base. Tuning is the value.** No reselling open source with a nice layer on top.
3. **Time is the scarce thing.** That's what you're buying back.
4. **Every price is on the page.** No "contact me for pricing". I price a product at roughly the tokens it took to build, mistakes included.

## How it's built

I write the spec; a fleet of AI agents does the build; a human stays in the loop at every step.
Every change is reviewed by a second, independent AI model before it lands.
